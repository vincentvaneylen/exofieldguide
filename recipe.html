How a picture is made

Every picture in the guide comes from the same 17 steps, run in this order. Each step says what goes in, the rule with its numbers, what comes out, when an observation replaces the rule, and how far to trust it. This is the only place the rules are written down; every other page links here.

Four words say where a number comes from. measured: read off a telescope observation. calculated: worked out from measured numbers by a stated rule. assumed: chosen because nothing measures it. imagined: decoration with no evidence either way.
